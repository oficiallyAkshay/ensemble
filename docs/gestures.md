# Gestures and settings

What each gesture does and what Settings can change. The overview is in the [README](../README.md).

## Gestures

| Do this | What happens |
|---|---|
| Look at a Claude session | That session is the target of every gesture. Looking at a different session while listening pauses dictation after 2 seconds, once your nose is at least 15 percent of that session's width (or height) inside it. |
| Pinch thumb and index twice, quickly | Starts listening through Claude's own dictation. Do it again to stop. Your hand can leave the frame in between. If Claude is already dictating in that session (started with its own button or shortcut), it stops that dictation instead, the same way, and the text counts as just dictated for a send. |
| Hold a thumbs-up | Sends the dictated draft. The default hold is 0.75 seconds, adjustable from 0.5 to 3 seconds in Settings. It works only within 30 seconds of dictation ending, whether Ensemble or Claude's own button started it. Made while dictating, it stops the dictation as soon as the thumbs-up is recognised and lets the transcript settle while the bar fills, so Send is pressed as the hold completes; drop your hand before then and the text stays, unsent ("Kept, not sent"). Made while Claude is dictating in the session on its own, it stops that dictation and sends once the transcript has settled. The island shows "Sending" from the end of the hold until "Sent" or "Not sent", as one step with one sound, and a thumbs-up still up after a send does not start a second hold. |
| Press the headphone stem once (Bluetooth output) | Same as the double pinch, in the session you are looking at: starts dictation, or stops it. Refused with the usual message when no session is targeted, Claude is not in front or the calibration is missing. |
| Press the headphone stem twice | Same as the thumbs-up: sends the dictated draft under the same rules. |
| Hold a peace sign (index and middle apart, ring and little folded, the thumb tucked or out) for 0.6 seconds | Widens the session you look at to the chat's default width, the same target as the fit described below (Claude's chat column plus 10 percent), and no further. The other sessions side by side with it give up only the width that takes, none below Claude's minimum, through as few dividers as possible; a session already that wide is left alone, and nothing has to be resized by hand afterwards. This works for any number of sessions side by side: Ensemble moves Claude's own dividers with the keyboard, each divider once, in one burst of keys. Unlike the fit, it ignores sessions fitted in the last three minutes, since you asked for it. The highlight goes straight to the session's new size as the resize starts. The pointer never moves. |
| Scissors: make a peace sign and close the two fingers together (to half their spread, or side by side) within 0.6 seconds | Evens out the sessions in that window: each gets an equal share of the width again, each divider moving once, in one quick burst of keys (a jump to its limit and Shift-arrow steps back where the target is nearer that limit than the divider is). A peace sign held long enough to widen the session does nothing more when it closes. |
| Pinch and drag up or down | Scrolls the session, at your system's scroll direction and speed. A fast release carries on with momentum, a slow release stops dead. While you scroll with the pinch held, the nose dot turns into the up-and-down arrows and turns back into the dot when the pinch releases; it does not move or change size. |
| Press Return while dictating | Stops the dictation, with the usual short tail so the last word is caught, and keeps the text unsent. Claude never sees that Return. Press Return again once it has stopped and Claude sends as it always does. Only while a dictation runs in the session you look at (Ensemble's own, or one Claude started that Ensemble has seen) and Claude is in front; Shift-Return and other modified Returns are left alone. |
| Press Return twice quickly while dictating | Stops, waits for the transcript to settle, then sends the draft under the thumbs-up's rules (Claude in front, a draft, nothing else typed or clicked since). A second Return while the stop is still in progress, or within 0.5 seconds of the first, counts. If the stop cannot be confirmed, nothing is sent and the island says "Not sent". Holding Return down is one press, never two, and its repeats never reach Claude until you let go. |

Looking at a session narrower than Claude's chat column widens it to 10 percent more than that column (the width past which Claude's text stops growing), never more, once your nose has rested inside it for 0.8 seconds with focus on it the whole time. The width comes through as few dividers as possible, usually one: from the wider neighbour when it can give it and stay at least as wide as the column, otherwise from the fewest sessions that can, and only when no session in the row has room above the column do others go below it, none below 300 points. A look passing over a session never resizes it, and it happens once each time you look at a session. If you look at another session while a fit runs, it stops and that session is fitted once you rest on it. Ensemble reads the column from a session wide enough to show it, and trusts a reading only when it is one of Claude's own columns (832, 1024 or 1344 points) or two sessions of different widths agree; until then it uses 832 points (Claude's default 768-point text column with its 32-point margins). A reading of the text alone (768, 960 or 1280 points) counts as that text with its margins, and the column is never taken as narrower than 832 points. Each divider moves once, in one quick burst of keys 2 ms apart (10 ms on a correcting pass): a jump to its limit first when the target is nearer that limit, then Shift-arrow steps of 32 points and at most two arrow steps of 8. The frames are read once afterwards, and a second burst follows only if a session landed more than 32 points off. A session fitted in the last three minutes keeps its width while the others can give the width; when they cannot, the session you rest on wins. Nothing is fitted for five seconds after a calibration, and a peace sign or scissors starts afresh. Never while you type or use the mouse (a fit waiting on that, a dictation, a send or a gesture runs once it clears, if you still rest there, and the log says once what it waited for), and Settings, Actions, "Fit the focused session to the chat width" turns it off. The peace sign and the scissors still work by hand.

The peace sign and the scissors act only with Claude in front, never while a dictation is running, starting or stopping or a send is in progress, and stop at once if Claude leaves the front, the divider loses the keyboard, or you touch the keyboard, mouse or trackpad. A key you type while a divider may hold the keyboard is held back, the resize stops, and the key goes to the message box once the keyboard is back there, so an arrow, Home or End of yours never moves a divider. Afterwards the keyboard goes back to the session's message box if a divider still holds it (a box you clicked into keeps it; with Claude not in front, when Claude comes back), and the highlight follows the session as it changes width. Ensemble's own keys never count as you typing, so they neither hold off focus nor refuse a later send; a key of yours meanwhile still does.

## Hands on the keyboard or mouse

While you type, click, scroll or move the pointer more than a few points, Ensemble steps back: keyboard focus holds and no window is raised, the highlight and nose dot fade out, and no session is resized. Gestures and headphone presses still work, on the session your nose is on: they are deliberate, and a hand raised from the keyboard to pinch lands inside the quiet time. The target keeps following your look through the pause, the highlight comes back on that session as soon as a gesture starts, and with your nose off every session a gesture is refused as usual. A double pinch or a scroll made right after typing acts; only a send is refused for 1.5 seconds after you type or click. The menu panel's status says "Focus holds while you type or use the mouse". Once the keyboard, mouse and trackpad have rested for 2 seconds (Settings, General, Pause after typing or mouse use: 1 to 5 seconds), the dot and highlight fade back in and keyboard focus moves to the session you look at. Within 1 second of that, or of Ensemble moving focus, one stray input reading does not pause again; a second one within half a second does. The camera and tracking keep running throughout, so resuming is instant; after 3 seconds paused the face tracker runs at 10 a second instead of every frame, and back at every frame on the next tick once you resume or a gesture starts. Only your own hardware input counts: the Returns Ensemble holds back while you dictate, the keys it posts (including the divider keys of a resize) and the pointer moves of its own scrolls never pause it. A pause also stops a resize in progress and drops a send asked for by gesture that had not gone out yet. Every dictation safety check keeps running.

When Ensemble starts a dictation, it also puts the keyboard in that session's message box, so a key you type while dictating lands there. It does this only while Claude is the app in front, and leaves focus alone otherwise.

Ensemble does no speech recognition of its own. The status capsule at the top of the screen, a floating one on screens without a notch, names the state and the session. Option-Command-K opens the control panel from anywhere. The panel, top to bottom: the on/off switch with the live status, the camera with its rate and the dot's ("camera 30 fps · dot 75 Hz": a webcam such as the C920 delivers at most 30 frames a second, while the dot moves on every refresh of its screen), how many screens are calibrated (click it to see your screens drawn where macOS has them, then click one to recalibrate only that screen), the nose dot and highlight switches, then Replay tour, the update button, Calibrate and Settings, and a footer with the installed version and a power button that quits. A refused gesture adds a short note under the camera that clears after a few seconds, or sooner once what it described no longer holds.

The six-step tour on first launch shows each gesture on a flat Claude window with one focused session and a 3D hand beside it, and lets you practise them with nothing reaching Claude. A later step asks whether to show the nose dot, which is off until you choose On. A panel under the 3D hand draws your own hand's 21 joints as the camera reads them; the joint points stay in the app, and no image is kept or sent. While the Headphone presses setting is on, the gestures step adds a fourth tab that explains the stem presses (Bluetooth output only) and checks it off if a press arrives; it is optional, can be skipped, and is never needed to finish. Replay it from the menu bar panel, the panel under Option-Command-K, or Settings.

## Warnings while you dictate

Only while dictating, and only when your look is heading out of the session you dictate into. The outer 15 percent of that session is the warning band.

| What lies beyond that edge | What you see |
|---|---|
| Another Claude session | The edge you share with it lights up blue, brighter as your nose nears it, with its name beside it. That session gets a dashed blue outline and a name chip, a thin dashed line runs from your session to your nose, and the island's session columns lean toward its column. Once your nose is across, the line turns amber and the shared edge fills amber as the 2-second switch wait runs. Looking back before it fills changes nothing. |
| Anything that is not Claude | The whole edge glows amber, brighter as your nose nears it. Turn it off with Settings, Actions, Warn when my look leaves Claude. |

Two guards apply only while dictating. The switch wait does not advance while you are speaking and resumes after about 0.6 seconds of quiet; it reads the input level, which exists only when live sound bars are on and the microphone is allowed, so without that the speaking guard is inactive. And a look counts as resting on another session only once your nose is 15 percent of its width (height for a session above or below) inside it.

## Settings

| Group | What you can change |
|---|---|
| General | Update automatically (off by default: checks soon after launch and then every hour, installs only while idle). Turn Ensemble on or off, open at login (restarts after a crash), play sounds (Claude's dictation start and stop sounds, and a send sound), pause other audio while dictating, use another microphone when the lid is closed (on by default: before each dictation the Mac's input moves to the webcam, a wired or a Bluetooth microphone, and goes back when the lid opens), and Pause after typing or mouse use (1 to 5 seconds, 2 by default). |
| Actions | One row per action, with the action's icon and the gesture that does it: Talk (pinch twice), Send (any pose, or Off; thumbs-up by default) with its hold time, and Scroll (pinch and move) with Reverse the scroll direction. Under "Other ways to scroll", Scroll up and Scroll down can each take a held pose; both are Off by default because pinch and move already scrolls, and a held pose would scroll whenever your hand rested in that shape. Under "Session widths", Maximize session (peace sign) and Even out sessions (scissors), each on by default and each can be Off; both stand aside while two fingers is another action's pose. Fit the focused session to the chat width (on by default). Warn when my look leaves Claude (on by default). Headphone presses on or off (on by default). |
| Display | Show the nose dot (off for new installs; installs that already had Ensemble keep their choice), highlight the session you face, pointer smoothing, correct for where I sit (on by default), live sound bars (asks for microphone access). |
| Privacy | Share anonymous usage (on by default; [what is sent](telemetry.md)), Show what would be sent, Reset install id. |
| Calibration | Calibrate from where you usually sit; sliding along the desk afterward needs no recalibration while "Correct for where I sit" is on (Settings, Display). Recalibrate after changing chairs, camera or monitor tilt. Settings, Calibration opens with your screens drawn where macOS has them (as System Settings, Displays draws them), each with its name, size and calibration date; click a screen, or its Recalibrate button below, to redo that screen only. Add position keeps a second calibration (standing, leaning back) beside the first; the one nearest your head is used, and Calibrate redoes that one. Each dot is captured only once your head has turned toward it and holds still. A new camera or screen asks on its own; while that card shows, a held thumbs-up starts it instead of sending. |
| About | Version, credits and the update button. Update automatically lives under General. The update button says "Check for Updates" (a found version opens a "What's new" window of the release's three to five items, Install or Later), "Update to x.y.z" once any check finds a new version (the same window; Install downloads, installs and reopens), "Installing x.y.z..." or "Installs x.y.z when you finish" while it works or waits for you to stop dictating, or "Up to date" for a few seconds after a check you started finds nothing. |

## Audio pause

While you dictate, other audio on speakers is paused with the play/pause key before the microphone opens, and resumes after the stop sound. The output volume is never touched, so Claude's sounds and Ensemble's start, stop and send sounds always play at your volume. Headphones are left alone. If the key does not stop the audio (it reached another app), the press is undone and the audio is left as it was. The setting is on by default.

## Headphone presses

macOS gives apps no "headphone tapped" event, so Ensemble listens the way a music player does: it registers for media remote commands, and macOS delivers a stem press only to the app it treats as Now Playing: a single press arrives as play/pause, a double press as next track (older AirPods' double-tap, if set to those). Ensemble claims Now Playing without playing any sound, using the microphone or asking for any extra permission, only while the setting is on, the output is Bluetooth, Claude is running and no other app is playing audio. If music starts, Ensemble lets go at once and presses control the music; it takes over again when the music stops. The press-and-hold mute goes to the app using the microphone, which is Claude, so Ensemble cannot use it. A play/pause key that Ensemble sends itself for the audio pause is ignored for 1.5 seconds so it can never count as a press.

## Hidden setting

`detailedTrace` writes a per-frame trace of numbers and states for tuning gestures, never images; each hand line carries the pose, what refused it (too far, angled), whether the hand has the two-fingers shape and the fingertip spread. Without it, the plain log still gets one line per two-fingers hold of 0.3 seconds or more: how long it was held, the spread's minimum, median and maximum, how often it read as two fingers or was refused, and whether a peace or scissors fired. It is off by default and rolls over at 20 MB. The command to turn it on is in the [contributing guide](../.github/CONTRIBUTING.md).
