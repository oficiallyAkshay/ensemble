A note from the person who made this

Type to your AI for thirty minutes and it can build two days of work. Talk to it for thirty minutes and it can build two weeks.

I learned that the slow way. I talk to my agents now, a lot of them, all at once. A dozen Claude sessions on three screens, each one waiting to hear what I want.

And my night became: find the right window, click it, talk. Find, click, talk.

So I got rid of the find and the click. I look at a session. I pinch. I talk. Thumbs-up. Next.

If you've already stopped typing to your agents, you're one click from leaning back.

How it works

No find.
No click.

01

Look.

Focus follows your nose. The session you face gets the blue outline.

02

Pinch twice.

Two quick pinches of thumb and index start Claude's own dictation.

03

Talk.

Say what you want. The words land in the session you are looking at.

Claude dictation onin api-refactordouble pinch to stop
04

Thumbs-up.

Hold it to send. Then look at the next session.

Download

01Open the download and drag Ensemble to Applications.
02Open it. macOS will say it is from an unidentified developer: go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
03Allow Camera and Accessibility, then follow the short tour.
Download for Mac

Needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14.2 or later, a webcam, and the Claude desktop app. Camera frames are read in memory and never stored or sent.
